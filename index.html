The models. Every DLSS model runs in the same NVIDIA DLSS library (version 310.9.16, bundled with the mod); the letter picks the neural network.
E: the convolutional (CNN) model of DLSS 3, the last one before the transformers: the softest and the cheapest.
J, K: DLSS 4, first-generation transformer model: sharper, more expensive (J and K measure the same here; NVIDIA recommends K).
L, M: DLSS 4.5, second-generation transformer model: the sharpest and cleanest, the most expensive (best on RTX 40/50).
FSR: AMD FSR 3.1.5 (the version this NVIDIA card runs; it works on any DirectX 12 GPU). The mod would use FSR 4 on Radeon RX 9000 cards, which is untested as of yet.
TAA: the game's own anti-aliasing, without the mod.

Shimmer is how much the image flickers from one frame to the next while your head is still. In VR you see it as crawling or sparkling on fine detail: foliage, wires, fences, thin edges, distant textures.
